# Simple Interest Calculator

## Overview

The Simple Interest Calculator is a simple tool used to calculate the interest earned or paid on a principal amount over a specific period at a fixed rate of interest.

## Objective

The objective of this project is to calculate simple interest quickly and accurately using a mathematical formula.

## Formula

Simple Interest (SI) = (P × R × T) / 100

Where:

* P = Principal amount
* R = Annual rate of interest (in percentage)
* T = Time period (in years)

## Example

Suppose:

* Principal (P) = ₹10,000
* Rate of Interest (R) = 5% per year
* Time (T) = 2 years

Calculation:

SI = (10,000 × 5 × 2) / 100

Simple Interest = ₹1,000

Total Amount = Principal + Simple Interest

Total Amount = ₹10,000 + ₹1,000 = ₹11,000

## Features

* Calculates simple interest using principal, rate, and time.
* Displays the calculated interest.
* Calculates the total amount payable.
* Easy to understand and use.

The Simple Interest Calculator helps users understand and calculate interest easily using a basic mathematical formula.
